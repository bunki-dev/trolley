# Help policy (demo store)

This is the demo store's customer help policy. The agent answers from it and the refund engine
enforces it. It is written for this project, not taken from any real retailer.

## P1 Missing items
If an item you paid for wasn't delivered, tell us within 7 days of delivery. We refund the item's
price to your original payment method. You don't need to send anything back.

## P2 Damaged, faulty or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, faulty, past its use-by date or isn't what you ordered, tell us within
7 days of delivery and we refund the item's price. You don't need to return it. For a refund over
$50 on one order, our team may ask for a photo before approving it.

## P3 Late delivery
If your delivery arrives more than 30 minutes after the end of your delivery window, we add a $10
credit to your account. One late credit per order.

## P4 Automatic refunds and our team
Trolley can approve refunds automatically up to $50 per order and $100 per customer in any 30 days.
Anything above that, anything older than 7 days, or anything we can't verify goes to our customer
care team, who reply within one business day.

## P5 Changing or cancelling an order
You can change the delivery window, add or remove items, or cancel for free until 10pm the night
before delivery. After that the order is being picked; contact our customer care team.

## P6 Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 3 to 5 business days.
Account credit is available on your next order straight away.

## P7 Food safety and allergic reactions
If you think a product made you or someone else unwell, or caused an allergic reaction, stop using it
and seek medical advice. In an emergency call 000. Report it and we pass it to our team immediately;
the assistant doesn't give medical advice.

## P8 Allergens and dietary information
Product information comes from the product's label data. Recipes and labels can change, so always
check the pack before eating if you have an allergy. "May contain" means the product could have
traces from shared equipment.

## P9 Substitutions
If an item is out of stock we can suggest a substitute. You choose whether to accept it. If a
substitute costs more, you pay the original item's price.

## P10 Delivery fees
Delivery is $5, free on orders of $100 or more.

## P11 Privacy
We only discuss orders on the account you're signed in to. We never share another customer's
details or orders.
